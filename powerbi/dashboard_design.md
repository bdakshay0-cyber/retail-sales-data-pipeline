# Retail Sales Dashboard

The Power BI report contains five analytical pages.

## 1. Executive Overview

KPIs:
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value

Visuals:
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Store
- Top 5 Products

Slicers:
- Year
- Month
- Category
- Store


## 2. Sales Trends

KPIs:
- Total Sales
- Average Monthly Sales
- Month-over-Month Growth
- Sales YTD

Visuals:
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Sales vs Previous Month
- Quarterly Sales
- Monthly Orders
- Units Sold Trend


## 3. Product Performance

KPIs:
- Total Sales
- Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Total Orders

Visuals:
- Sales by Category
- Units by Category
- Top Products
- Bottom Products
- Product Performance Matrix

Matrix hierarchy:
Category → Product

Conditional formatting:
- Data bars on Total Sales
- Product-level ranking


## 4. Store Performance

KPIs:
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value

Visuals:
- Sales by Store
- Orders by Store
- Units Sold by Store
- Monthly Store Trend
- Store Ranking Matrix


## 5. Customer Analysis

KPIs:
- Total Customers
- Total Sales
- Sales per Customer
- Orders per Customer

Visuals:
- Top Customers
- Customer Order Frequency
- Sales by City
- Customers by City
- Customer Performance Matrix
